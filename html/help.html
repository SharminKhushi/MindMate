Help & Guide

New here, or not sure how something works? Answers to the most common questions below.

Getting Started

How do I create an account?

Go to the Sign Up page, enter your name, age (13-19), email, and a password of at least 6 characters. That's it — your private space is ready instantly.

Is my data private?

Yes. Your journal, mood history, goals, and contacts are stored securely and are only accessible to your own account — no one else, including other users, can see them.

Journal & Mood

How do I write a journal entry?

Go to Journal, click "Add Entry", write freely, pick how you felt (one or more moods), and save. You can edit or delete any entry later.

What does the Mood Check-in do with my answers?

Based on your mood, stress, energy, and sleep, MindMate gives you one gentle, relevant suggestion — like a breathing exercise or a self-help topic — right after you check in.

Goals, Habits & Challenges

How does goal progress work?

Each goal has a progress percentage you update yourself with the +10%/-10% buttons — there's no automatic tracking, so you're always in control.

What happens if I miss a day on a Challenge?

Nothing bad! You get 1 free "rest day" each week that keeps your streak alive even if you miss a day — because taking a break is part of being healthy, not a failure.

Privacy & Account

Can I delete my account and all my data?

Yes. Go to Profile & Settings → Privacy & Data → Delete My Account. This permanently removes your account and everything linked to it — this can't be undone.
